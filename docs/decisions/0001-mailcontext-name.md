# 0001: Use MailContext as the working product name

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-28

## Context

The product needs a name that is understandable, memorable, and capable of
representing a high-quality general email-copying tool. Literal combinations of
“mail” and “copy” communicate the action well but are generic, crowded, and
frequently ambiguous or already used.

## Decision

Use **MailContext** as the working product and repository name.

The initial descriptor is:

> Copy one message or the whole thread—clean, complete, and ready to paste.

## Rationale

“Mail” makes the domain immediately clear. “Context” describes the useful,
portable result and creates an association with AI-assisted work without
restricting the product to AI or to a particular destination.

The name can encompass technical documentation, chats, notes, CRMs, and other
emails. It also showed better apparent distinctiveness than the most literal
alternatives during the initial naming research.

## Consequences

- Product copy and UI must make the central **copy** action explicit.
- The name should not cause the roadmap to expand into general mailbox context
  or AI search prematurely.

## Alternatives considered

- **CopyMail:** semantically strong but associated with an existing adjacent
  email product and crowded digital namespace.
- **CopyEmail:** clear but generic and easily interpreted as copying an email
  address.
- **MailCopy:** crowded and ambiguous with email copywriting.
- **MailClip / CleanCopy / MailCopier:** pre-existing uses and weak
  distinctiveness.
- **MailPaste / CopyMyMail / MailCopyPaste:** more available but less precise or
  less suitable for a polished product identity.
